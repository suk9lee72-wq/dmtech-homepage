Common Repair Flow

01

Incoming Inspection

Visual inspection, contamination check, and condition verification.

02

Analysis & Quotation

Repair feasibility review, scope confirmation, and lead time estimation.

03

Repair / Cleaning

Cleaning, polishing, surface restoration, and repair coordination.

04

Final QC

Outgoing inspection, dimensional check, leak test, and QC review.

05

Shipment

Packaging support and domestic or international shipment handling.

Key Inspection Points by Part

ESC

Chucking force, flatness, surface condition, dielectric condition, and contamination.

Ceramic

Crack inspection, surface contamination, chipping, polishing condition, and visual QC.

Shower Head

Hole blockage, surface condition, cleaning status, flatness, and contamination.

Vacuum Parts

Leak test, sealing area condition, vacuum cleanliness, and surface damage.
